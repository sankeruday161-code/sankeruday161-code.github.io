About Me

I am a cybersecurity professional with an Advanced Diploma in Cyber Security from Blitz Academy and training in Vulnerability Assessment & Penetration Testing (VAPT). I work with industry-standard tools such as Burp Suite, Nmap, Nessus, OpenVAS, Wireshark and Metasploit, and I have working knowledge of the OWASP Top 10 and the MITRE ATT&CK framework, including how VAPT findings are reported.

I am also familiar with SIEM platforms (Splunk, Wazuh, Microsoft Sentinel) and use Python, Bash and PowerShell to automate tasks.

Before moving into security, I spent two years working as an Accountant. That experience gave me strong attention to detail and disciplined record keeping, which I now bring to security testing and documentation.
